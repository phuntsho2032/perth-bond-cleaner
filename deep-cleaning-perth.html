What is Deep Cleaning?

Deep cleaning is an intensive cleaning service that goes beyond routine maintenance. It focuses on removing accumulated dirt, grease, dust, bacteria, and grime from areas that are often overlooked during regular cleaning.

Unlike standard cleaning, deep cleaning targets build-up on surfaces, inside cupboards, behind appliances, and in high-traffic zones that collect hidden debris over time.

It's an ideal choice for homeowners, tenants, and businesses who want a fresh start or need a property thoroughly refreshed.

Our deep cleaning service covers kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, bedrooms, and high-touch surfaces, leaving your property genuinely clean and hygienic.

Deep Cleaning vs Standard Cleaning – What's the Difference?

Standard cleaning maintains a property during everyday use. It covers routine tasks like vacuuming, mopping, and surface wiping.

Deep cleaning goes much further. It targets hidden build-up, grease, stains, and grime that accumulate over time in areas that are often overlooked.

What is deep cleaning?

How is deep cleaning different from regular cleaning?

How much does deep cleaning cost in Perth?
