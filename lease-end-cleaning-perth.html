Understanding Lease-End Cleaning

Lease-end cleaning, also known as end of lease cleaning or vacate cleaning, is a comprehensive top-to-bottom clean completed before handing a rental property back to the owner or property manager.

The goal of a professional lease-end clean is to help tenants prepare the property for final inspection while reducing the risk of cleaning-related disputes and bond deductions.

Perth property managers and real estate agencies often follow detailed inspection checklists covering kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, windows, walls, and high-touch areas throughout the property.

Our Perth lease-end cleaning services are designed around common real estate inspection standards to help ensure homes are presented in clean, inspection-ready condition.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is lease-end cleaning?

Lease-end cleaning, also known as end of lease cleaning or vacate cleaning, is a detailed clean completed before handing a rental property back to the landlord or property manager. It covers every room in the property and is designed to help tenants prepare for final inspection.

How much does lease-end cleaning cost in Perth?

Pricing depends on the size of the property, its condition, and any additional services required. Contact us for a customised quote based on your property details.

What is the difference between lease-end cleaning and regular cleaning?

Lease-end cleaning is a detailed inspection-focused clean completed before moving out of a rental property. It covers areas that are not usually included in standard weekly cleaning.

Do I need carpet steam cleaning for my rental property?

Many Perth lease agreements require professional carpet steam cleaning, especially if pets were kept inside the property. We can arrange this service if needed.

How long does a lease-end clean take?

The timeframe depends on the property size and condition. Smaller apartments may take several hours, while larger homes can require a full day of cleaning.

Do you service suburbs outside Perth City?

Yes. We provide lease-end cleaning services across Perth City and surrounding suburbs including Subiaco, Morley, Fremantle, Joondalup, Baldivis, Canning Vale, and many more.

Should the property be empty before cleaning?

Yes. For best results, the property should be fully vacated before the cleaning appointment so all areas can be properly accessed and cleaned.
