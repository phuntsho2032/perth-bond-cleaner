What is Rental Property Cleaning?

Rental property cleaning is a flexible service that caters to the needs of tenants, landlords, and property managers throughout the rental lifecycle.

It covers move-out cleaning for tenants, move-in preparation for new occupants, and periodic cleaning for ongoing rental management.

Perth's rental market is competitive, and properties that are clean and well-presented attract better tenants and smoother transitions.

Whether you're handing back keys or welcoming new tenants, our cleaning service is built around the specific requirements of rental properties.

Rental Cleaning for Every Stage

Our rental property cleaning services are flexible enough to cover move-out cleans, move-in preparation, and ongoing maintenance for investment properties.

Every property is different, and we tailor our approach to suit the size, condition, and specific requirements of each rental.

Move-Out Cleaning for Tenants

Moving out of a rental property comes with expectations from landlords and agents. Our move-out cleaning service focuses on leaving the property in top condition.

We pay attention to kitchens, bathrooms, floors, windows, and often-overlooked areas like cupboards, skirting boards, and light fittings.

Move-In Cleaning for New Tenants

Moving into a fresh property is exciting, but it's not always as clean as expected. Our move-in cleaning service helps new tenants settle into a spotless environment.

We also work with landlords and property managers to prepare properties before new occupants arrive, ensuring a positive first impression.

Kitchen & Living Areas

Kitchens receive detailed attention, including oven degreasing, stovetop cleaning, cupboard wiping, and sink sanitisation.

Living areas, dining rooms, and hallways are vacuumed, mopped, and dusted to remove everyday build-up.

Bathroom & Laundry

Bathrooms and laundries are thoroughly sanitised. We tackle soap scum, mould, grout stains, and hard water marks to restore a clean, fresh appearance.

Mirrors, basins, toilets, and shower screens are polished and left spotless.

Bedrooms & Storage Areas

We clean wardrobes, shelves, skirting boards, door frames, and window sills throughout the property. Dust and cobwebs are removed from ceilings, corners, and light fittings.

Every room is left fresh and well-maintained.

Flooring Throughout

Vacuuming and mopping are included as standard across all floor types.

Carpet steam cleaning is also available as an optional add-on for properties that require it.

Windows & Glass

Internal windows, tracks, and frames are cleaned to enhance natural light and overall presentation.

Garages & Outdoor Areas

Garages, sheds, and outdoor spaces are often overlooked during rental transitions. We include basic cleaning of these areas to ensure the entire property is accounted for.

From sweeping floors to wiping down shelving, we help leave these spaces tidy and presentable.
