What is Vacate House Cleaning?

Vacate house cleaning is a comprehensive move-out service performed before returning a rental property to the landlord or agent. It goes far beyond a standard weekly tidy, focusing on deep-cleaning every room, fixture, and surface.

The primary goal is to present the house in excellent condition so that the final walkthrough proceeds smoothly and without dispute.

Perth real estate agents and landlords follow detailed checklists covering kitchens, bathrooms, floors, windows, and walls. Our vacate cleaning process aligns with these expectations.

Whether you're leaving a two-bedroom unit or a four-bedroom family home, we tailor the cleaning to your specific property and its requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is vacate house cleaning?

Vacate house cleaning is a thorough move-out clean completed before handing a rental house back to the landlord. It covers every area to ensure the property is presented in excellent condition.

How much does vacate cleaning cost for a house in Perth?

Costs depend on the size of the house, its current condition, and any extra services such as carpet steam cleaning. We provide free custom quotes tailored to your property.

What's the difference between vacate cleaning and regular house cleaning?

Regular cleaning maintains a home during daily living. Vacate cleaning is far more comprehensive, targeting areas like oven interiors, window tracks, skirting boards, and cupboards that are often missed during routine cleaning.

Do I need professional carpet cleaning for my vacate clean?

Many Perth leases require professional carpet steam cleaning as part of the vacate process, especially if pets have been kept inside. We can arrange this if needed.

How long does a vacate house clean take?

The duration depends on the size and condition of the house. A standard family home may take several hours to a full day to complete thoroughly.

Do you clean houses in suburbs outside Perth City?

Yes. We provide vacate cleaning across Perth and surrounding suburbs including Subiaco, Morley, Fremantle, Joondalup, Baldivis, Canning Vale, and many more.

Should the house be empty before you arrive?

Yes. For the best results, the property should be cleared of all furniture and personal items so our team can access every area without obstacles.
