Preparation Guide

For Tenants

  • Remove all personal belongings and rubbish
  • Ensure utilities (power, water) are connected
  • Provide keys or access codes on cleaning day
  • Remove any items blocking access to areas
  • Take photos before and after for your records

For Property Managers

  • Schedule inspection after cleaning completion
  • Provide detailed report if issues found
  • Allow 24-48 hours for cleaning completion
  • Ensure clear access for cleaning team
  • Coordinate key collection/return if needed
